Privacy Policy for SnapFull — Full Page Screenshot

Last updated: September 17, 2026

SnapFull ("the extension") is a browser extension that captures, crops, redacts, and exports full-page screenshots. This privacy policy explains how the extension handles data.

No Data Collection

SnapFull does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, browsing history, website content, or usage data. The extension operates entirely on your local device.

No Cloud, No Servers, No Accounts

SnapFull has no backend server. It does not require an account, login, or internet connection to function. Screenshots you capture, crop, or redact stay on your device and are never uploaded anywhere.

Permissions Explained

  • activeTab — used only when you click the extension icon, to capture the currently visible page.
  • scripting — used to measure page height and stitch together the full-page image, only during an active capture.
  • downloads — used to save your exported screenshot (PNG/PDF) to your device.
  • offscreen — used to process and compose the captured image without disrupting your browsing.
  • storage / unlimitedStorage — used only to save your local preferences and temporarily hold large captures before export, on your own device.

Third Parties

SnapFull does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. No data is sold, shared, or transferred to any third party.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the developer via the Chrome Web Store listing page.
